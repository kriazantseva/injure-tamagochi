This project was created to experiment with enum classes, enum structures, and explore their functionality. 
It is a Pet Simulator featuring various indicators that reflect hunger, energy, well-being, and more. 
The pet has its own mood, which changes based on specific actions and its current state. Randomizers are used to 
determine the pet’s favorite diet and toys, meaning feeding it certain types of food or playing with specific 
toys will influence its mood.

At this stage, it is just a draft, but it is intended to evolve into a full-fledged simulator in the future.

Several key points necessary for creating the simulator:

There should be multiple animals to choose from, differing not only in appearance but also in characteristics.
Several ways to interact with the pet, starting with the most basic ones.
A mechanism for increasing and decreasing indicators.

The pet's basic needs:

Food
Play
Attention
Walks
Shower
Sleep

Indicators:

Hunger
Well-being
Energy
Mood
Cleanliness

In some cases, choosing the wrong care strategy should affect the pet's well-being and mood, potentially leading to destructive behaviors. In the future, this could increase the irritability level, prompting the pet to make internet requests, open the console, or send requests via Wi-Fi.

Pet creation process:

Entering your name.
Choosing a pet from the shelter.
Naming the pet.

Pet-specific indicators:

Life level
Hunger rate
Mood changes
Irritability level
Damage level
